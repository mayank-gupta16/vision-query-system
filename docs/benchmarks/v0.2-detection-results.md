# v0.2 detector/runtime benchmark results

Issue [#21](https://github.com/mayank-gupta16/vision-query-system/issues/21)
selects `vehicle-detection-0201` with OpenVINO 2026.3.1 on CPU and a confidence
threshold of `0.95` as the v0.2 evaluation baseline. This is an adapter direction,
not approval to ship the runtime or weights.

## Decision

`vehicle-detection-0200` is ineligible because its 60.00% easy-stratum precision
misses the frozen 80.00% floor. Both larger candidates pass every absolute gate.
Among those passing candidates, `0201` provides 26.424 frames per CPU-second,
1.98 times `0202`'s 13.325, while retaining 100.00% precision and meeting every
recall floor. `0202` improves overall recall from 83.33% to 100.00%, but halves
CPU-normalized throughput, halves wall throughput, lowers precision, and adds
memory without changing ranked AP50 on this set. The smaller `0201` is therefore
the better reversible v0.2 accuracy/CPU tradeoff.

The selected threshold is `0.95`, chosen solely on the locked calibration split
by the predeclared F1/floor/tie-break rule. The test split was evaluated only
after that threshold was fixed.

## Limits and next boundary

The 12-item test split is deliberately small, vehicle-only, source-separated,
and privacy-sanitized. Its synthetic backgrounds are less complex than real
traffic scenes, AP50 saturates on all three candidates, and multiple source
photos depict related vehicle brands. These measurements do not establish
pedestrian, bicycle, weather, camera-domain, demographic, long-video, or
production accuracy.

The next research may use `0201` and threshold `0.95` as its locked baseline.
Product integration still needs a Detector port contract, an adapter issue,
real-media boundary validation, dependency/weight shipping review, and the
downstream sampling, crop-value, and tracking results. No model, runtime, source
image, or derived frame is added to the V1 release by this decision.
