# v0.2 short-term tracking research methodology

Issue [#24](https://github.com/mayank-gupta16/vision-query-system/issues/24)
compares bounded, appearance-free tracklet policies under stationary and moving
cameras, hard cuts, occlusion, and dense crossings on CPU-LITE. It evaluates
research adapters only. It does not add a shipping tracker, model, media asset,
cross-clip identity mechanism, or persistent entity ReID to the package.

## Question, candidates, and preregistered selection

The question is whether last-box or two-observation constant-velocity geometry
preserves clip-local vehicle identity when both use exact maximum-total-IoU
one-to-one association. Both candidates start a tracklet immediately from a
detector observation and emit only detector-supported boxes.

[`candidates.json`](../../fixtures/v02-tracking-research/candidates.json) locks
54 calibration configurations before test inference:

- tracker family: global last-box IoU or global velocity IoU;
- association IoU: `0.10`, `0.30`, or `0.50`;
- maximum consecutive missed 5 FPS samples: `0`, `2`, or `5`;
- image-discontinuity cut threshold: `0.15`, `0.25`, or `0.35` mean absolute
  RGB difference.

Association happens before a miss is charged. A track terminates when its miss
count becomes `max_missed_samples + 1`; its end PTS remains its last
detector-supported observation. A detected cut terminates every active track
before the first post-cut association. End of source terminates the remaining
tracklets. Cut, miss-timeout, and source-end termination counts are retained.

For each family, only configurations passing every calibration gate are
eligible. They are ordered by higher overall HOTA, higher overall IDF1, higher
worst hard-stratum HOTA, fewer overall switches, fewer overall fragments,
fewer allowed misses, higher IoU threshold, and lower cut threshold. The
generated calibration-selection lock permits exactly one finalist per family.
Test compares only those finalists. Among passing test finalists, the result is
ordered by overall HOTA, IDF1, worst hard-stratum HOTA, switch rate,
fragmentation rate, real-time factor, then name. If neither passes, there is no
selection; gates are not weakened.

## Locked dataset, rights, and privacy

The dataset contains fifteen deterministic 12-second, 640 x 360 RGB24 rawvideo
MOV sequences with 60 exact 200 ms frames each. Five calibration clips and ten
source-family-disjoint test clips provide one and two complete sequences,
respectively, for each scenario:

- stationary camera control with three moving vehicles;
- deterministic pan and shake with three vehicles;
- two hard cuts with new ground-truth occurrence identities after each cut;
- one-, three-, and five-sample full occlusions of three continuing identities
  in disjoint x-lanes, isolating gap recovery from inter-vehicle layering. Each
  adjacent partial frame masks the middle 56 of 170 columns, so the annotation
  records the exact floored visible fraction, `114 / 170 = 670588 / 1000000`;
- six simultaneous same-appearance and mirrored vehicle crossings in three
  lanes. Taller occurrences are painted first within a lane, exact visible
  area is recorded, and generation fails if any scored box falls below 25%
  visibility.

Each clip has one primary scenario. Metrics for a hard stratum combine the
complete sequences assigned to that stratum; `overall` combines every complete
sequence. Cuts are not split into new metric sequences because doing so would
hide cross-cut association errors. Ground-truth and predicted IDs are unique
within a full clip and never reused after cut reset.

The generator uses only the ten plate-sanitized CC0 issue #21 derivatives and
project-generated backgrounds. A source family may create multiple scenarios
inside one split but never crosses from calibration to test. Track IDs identify
rendered occurrences, not real entities. Generated scenes contain no real
person, face, retained plate, personal data, text, font, or unrecorded imported
asset.

scenarios. [`annotations.json`](../../fixtures/v02-tracking-research/annotations.json)
binds every clip byte hash, RGB frame hash, PTS, cut, occurrence ID, box,
visibility state, and complete-sequence stratum. The
[`dataset manifest`](../../fixtures/v02-tracking-research/dataset-manifest.json)
binds independent split payload and occurrence-ID hashes. Their SHA-256 values

- source: `b15732e4a22e816c718cb1ea5a210660532b15fd31c72b9f24bd39c153427b5f`;
- annotations: `657965bafc568e6c9fb78539fd5b6612ed876c48013d9b698c1a567df1c64cc7`;

Source PPMs, generated MOVs, extracted frames, detector outputs, model weights,
runtime wheels, and inspection images remain ignored. Committed raw evidence is
pixel-free and bounded to opaque sequence IDs, PTS, boxes, track IDs, scores,
counts, timings, stable failures, and hashes.

## Candidate boundary and cut guard

The detector worker receives opaque frame and source identifiers, pixels, and a
dummy non-ground-truth box. The tracker receives only an opaque sequence
boundary and chronological records containing exact PTS, detector boxes, and a
candidate-independent image-discontinuity score. It never receives split,
scenario, ground-truth identity, annotated cut, visibility, or occlusion data.

The discontinuity guard samples every fourth RGB pixel and reports the mean
absolute channel delta in integer basis points of 255. Its CPU cost is included.
Cut thresholds are calibrated on image scores; annotated cut indices are used
only later by the metric evaluator.

## Metric contract

The minimal MIT-licensed implementation in
[`v02_tracking_metrics.py`](../../scripts/v02_tracking_metrics.py) adapts the
official TrackEval HOTA and Identity semantics without importing TrackEval,
SciPy, its dataset readers, logging, plotting, or CLI. The design reference is
TrackEval commit `12c8791b303e0a0b50f753af204249e622d0281a`: HOTA source SHA-256
`c582255c3d36bdb49c3ceeb2a5bd912cee009190dfcfe465b4173d03aaa97d6a`,
Identity source SHA-256
`e76b1bdcbf5b193662431596a984cf70a5be32e903eb5f2a3d9921b4762f93ba`,
and MIT license SHA-256
`f7e548c5729e464d878dd4abf691e323bbea028a756d08395d88b83e43dd2d86`.
At protocol lock, the local metric implementation SHA-256 is
`2f3bdb3b207e83202f9bd2d1fb12cf19d19c2c8c60412201ac3ffaab6b7ee2a8`.

- HOTA uses vehicle-box IoU and inclusive alpha thresholds `0.05` through
  `0.95`, with the pinned implementation's binary64 machine-epsilon boundary.
  Sequence TP/FN/FP and association counts are combined before each alpha
  result. The reported value is the floor of 10,000 times the mean of the
  nineteen HOTA values, never the average of per-sequence percentages.
- IDF1 uses inclusive IoU `0.50`, an exact global identity assignment for each
  complete sequence, summed IDTP/IDFN/IDFP, and a floor to basis points.
- A switch occurs when a visible GT occurrence's assigned predicted ID differs
  from its last assigned ID. Its last match survives unmatched and fully
  occluded frames but never a cut because GT occurrence IDs terminate there.
- A fragmentation is `matched visible runs - 1`. Fully occluded unscored frames
  neither open nor close a run; initial late acquisition is not a fragment.
- Switches and fragmentations divide by visible GT object-frame annotations and
  round upward per 1,000. An empty denominator is an error.
- A false cut continuation is each unique clip/cut/predicted ID emitted in both
  adjacent 20-frame cut segments. Any nonzero value fails.

The evaluator rejects duplicate frame IDs, non-integer boxes, non-finite
weights, more than 64 objects per frame, more than 512 identities or 100,000
observations per metric input, and oversized or malformed assignments. Goldens
cover perfect, empty, split, switched, fragmented, occluded, tied, exact-IoU,
and cut-reset behavior.

## Repetitions, timing, and isolation

Calibration and test each receive one excluded warm-up followed by seeds
`1729`, `3253`, `5081`, `7919`, and `104729`. The CLI refuses test inference
and evaluation until a calibration result and exact finalist lock are present.
The committed test annotations remain intentionally review-visible, but neither
the detector worker nor tracker receives their oracle fields. The barrier
revalidates all 54 configurations, five seeds, aggregates, gates, and selected
family winners. It binds the candidate, data, policy, source revision,
calibration result, harness, metric, dataset validator and its generation
helpers, detector worker, sampling launcher, and gate evaluator hashes.

Integrated wall/CPU time includes clip verification and decode, PPM staging,
detector preprocessing and inference, source-coordinate output, the image cut
guard, association, and termination. Fixture generation, metric evaluation,
receipt construction, and output writes are excluded. The receipt reports
source-seconds per wall-second, peak parent/worker RSS, detector and tracker
timings, detection count, maximum active tracks, and termination counts.

The exact CPython/OpenVINO/NumPy closure from issue #21 is reconstructed from
verified wheels. Detection runs under clear-environment bubblewrap with no
network, read-only exact runtime/model/data mounts, private tmpfs, bounded
output, and timeout. Tracker and metric code are reviewed in-process source;
third-party TrackEval is never downloaded or executed by the harness.

## Reproduction boundary

Generate the ignored clips from the exact issue #21 derivative root:

```sh
python3 scripts/prepare_v02_tracking_dataset.py \
  --input-root artifacts/issue21/dataset-boundaryfix-2 \
  --output artifacts/issue24/dataset-v18
```

Run calibration from the reviewed protocol commit, then install and review the
generated calibration result and selection lock before any test run:

```sh
python3 scripts/run_v02_tracking_benchmark.py \
  --phase calibration \
  --dataset-root artifacts/issue24/dataset-v18 \
  --models-root artifacts/issue21/models \
  --base-python artifacts/toolchain/3.13.15/python/\
cpython-3.13.15-linux-x86_64-gnu/bin/python3.13 \
  --wheels-root artifacts/issue21/wheels \
  --source-revision REVIEWED_PROTOCOL_COMMIT \
  --evaluated-on 2026-09-08 \
  --output artifacts/issue24/calibration
```

After the committed selection lock is independently reviewed, use the same
command with `--phase test` and a new output root. Existing or linked output
roots are refused. Test output includes content-addressed receipts, immediate
frozen-gate evaluations, raw metric components, pixel-free representative
predictions/tracklets, bounded examples of visible misses, identity switches,
fragmentation reacquisitions, and cross-cut continuations, exact environment
closure, and provenance hashes.

## Interpretation limits

This is a small vehicle-only, synthetically composed dataset. It challenges
geometric association and termination but does not estimate pedestrian,
bicycle, weather, compression, real-camera, long-video, demographic, or
persistent-identity performance. Rectangular source context remains around the
vehicle chips. A passing result can select a later V0.2 adapter contract; it is
not a V1 shipping claim or approval to distribute the evaluated model/runtime.
