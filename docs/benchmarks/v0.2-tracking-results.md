# v0.2 short-term tracking benchmark results

Issue [#24](https://github.com/mayank-gupta16/vision-query-system/issues/24)
selects `global-last-iou` as the bounded v0.2 short-term vehicle-tracklet
policy. Both calibration-locked finalists pass every frozen test gate, but the
last-box candidate wins the preregistered lexicographic ranking at its first
criterion, overall HOTA. It also leads overall IDF1 and identity switches,
while the velocity candidate leads worst hard-stratum HOTA. This is a ratified
input to adapter design, not a claim that a production tracker or persistent
ReID is already shipped.

## Decision

Adopt the following reference behavior for the v0.2 implementation boundary:

- exact global maximum-total-IoU one-to-one association;
- the last detector-supported box as the association geometry;
- a `0.10` IoU threshold;
- at most five missed 5 FPS samples before termination on the sixth miss;
- a `0.15` mean absolute RGB discontinuity threshold that terminates every
  active track before the first post-cut association;
- detector-supported observations only, with no extrapolated output;
- identities scoped to one clip and no persistent entity ReID.

The selected candidate reaches 8,217 HOTA and 8,580 IDF1 basis points overall,
with 8 identity switches and 24 fragmentations per 1,000 visible track frames.
It has zero false continuation across cuts, zero failed repetitions, 4.251x
real-time throughput, and a worst peak RSS of 589,635,584 bytes (562.32 MiB) on
CPU-LITE.

The velocity finalist also passes, but ranks second: 8,083 HOTA, 8,151 IDF1,
12 switches, 24 fragmentations, zero false cut continuation, zero failures,
and 4.250x real-time throughput. Its dense-crossing HOTA is 772 basis points
higher, but the last-box policy is 1,701 points higher under camera motion and
398 higher under occlusion. The simpler last-box policy therefore has the
stronger measured default boundary.

## Production handoff

`visualworld.tracking.GlobalLastBoxTracker` implements this selected boundary
behind the version-1 `Tracker` port. Its canonical configuration digest is
`e5a8fd8bdd43abeeb93b1602668be2436ce8f6c8553d9002c10038526e41ece2`.
The regression oracle replays all ten committed representative held-out
sequences and requires the production adapter to reproduce the research
tracker's complete observation partitions and `cut`, `miss_timeout`, and
`source_end` termination counts. This handoff adds no model, runtime, media, or
pixel artifact.

## Calibration and one-shot test integrity

The first calibration attempt failed closed before any test inference because
the original occlusion composition unintentionally measured vehicle layering.
The dataset was revised symmetrically across calibration and test into three
disjoint lanes. Independent review then found that a partial mask left exactly
114 of 170 columns visible while its annotation claimed 65%; visibility was
corrected to the exact floored `670588 / 1000000`, with mask geometry and color
added to the source lock. The candidate grid, gates, tracker, and metrics were
not changed.

The corrected protocol was reviewed and frozen at commit
`4bf8154354dd89a6d34f59b9a90aa8a870c18b8c`. Official calibration evaluated
all 54 configurations over seeds `1729`, `3253`, `5081`, `7919`, and `104729`.
Five configurations passed every calibration gate; the preregistered ordering
locked exactly one finalist per family in commit
`0721625796ea340b2c22914fdddf1149d6dc9f76`. Independent reviewers recomputed
the aggregates, dispersions, gates, eligible sets, winners, and trusted-code
hash closure before test was unlocked.

The test phase then ran once, using exactly the two committed finalists. All
five detector repetitions produced the same prediction SHA-256
`6a11ed734d3bacaf2b5931196dd3217867ec146e4ac07d24cfad43a19ecbd6ba`;
each candidate's accuracy metrics were identical across seeds. Resource timing
varied as expected: RTF median absolute deviation was 0.133x for last-box and
0.132x for velocity, while peak-RSS dispersion was 307,200 bytes.

## Limits and next boundary

The recommendation covers one vehicle detector at confidence 0.95, fixed 5
FPS, ten short synthetic held-out clips, and one CPU profile. It does not
establish pedestrian, bicycle, weather, compression, real-camera, long-video,
cross-clip identity, or persistent ReID behavior. Five deterministic seeds
confirm execution repeatability; they are not five independently sampled test
datasets and do not provide a population confidence interval.

The next v0.2 work should use this result to decompose roadmap epic #30 into a
small shipping detector/tracklet vertical slice. That implementation must keep
the reference policy clip-local, preserve source coordinates and evidence,
return explicit unsupported or uncertain states outside the measured boundary,
and keep model/runtime distribution optional until its license and packaging
gate is separately accepted.
