# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We will act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Show empathy and respect for differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the community and project.
- Respect privacy, consent, and the sensitive nature of visual data.
- Never publish private media, credentials, or identifying personal data.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, trolling, sexualized attention,
publishing another person's private information, or other conduct reasonably
considered inappropriate in a professional setting is unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Broad community contribution intake is not open yet. A dedicated private conduct
contact and full enforcement procedure must be published before it opens. Do not
post sensitive conduct reports in public issues. GitHub-hosted abuse should use
GitHub's platform reporting/blocking tools in the interim. Maintainers may remove,
edit, or reject contributions and restrict participation when behavior violates
this policy.
